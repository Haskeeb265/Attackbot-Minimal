# Vulnerability Disclosure Policy

We take the security of our systems seriously and value the contributions of the security community. This policy outlines how to responsibly report vulnerabilities to us and what you can expect in return.

## Reporting Guidelines

If you believe you have discovered a vulnerability in our systems, products, or services, we encourage you to report it to us privately and responsibly.

Please include the following details in your report:

- A detailed description of the vulnerability
- The steps required to reproduce the issue
- Any relevant URLs, payloads, screenshots, or logs
- The potential impact and any suggested remediation

We aim to responding in 5 days, triaging within 10 days and resolution in 30 days.


**Ineligible:**

- Social engineering attacks (e.g., phishing, vishing)
- Physical security vulnerabilities
- Denial of service (DoS/DDoS)
- Vulnerabilities in third-party software or services not maintained by us
- Reports based on automated scans without clear evidence of exploitability
- 3rd party password leaks (except employee passwords)

## Exclusions

To remain eligible for recognition or rewards:

- Do **not** make any vulnerability or associated data public without our prior written consent
- Do **not** violate any laws or disrupt services during testing
- Do **not** access, modify, or delete any data that does not belong to you

## What You Can Expect

- We will respond to your report promptly
- We will not take legal action against you for good faith security research
- We will keep you informed about the status of your report
- If eligible, you may receive recognition and a bounty, depending on the severity and impact of the vulnerability

We appreciate your efforts to keep our systems secure and thank you for your contribution to improving our security posture.
